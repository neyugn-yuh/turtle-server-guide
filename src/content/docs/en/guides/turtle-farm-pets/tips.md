## 1. Where do I get my first egg?

Bosses may drop **Trứng Lửa**. Reward crates can also grant eggs if server includes that reward. By default you do not need a pet to encounter monsters, though server may change requirement. Harvest or cut trees in supported areas, fight monsters yourself and advance boss progress.

:::caution[Not confirmed]
Plugin does not identify which specific server crates contain eggs or how you obtain their keys. Ask administrator which egg sources are available.
:::

## 2. Does incubation stop while offline?

No. Incubation uses elapsed time. Once ready, open Lò ấp and click egg; with ranch scene active and owner online, it may also hatch automatically. Each incubator holds one egg at a time. Full pens do not lose your new pet: it enters Kho pet.

## 3. Can hungry pets die or lose buffs?

Hunger handling stops pets firing at zero satiety; it does not delete them. Hunger does not disable buffs by default, but server may change this. Penned pets lose satiety while offline and spend more when firing. Withdrawing to storage stops time-based loss, buffs and combat support.

## 4. Why is my pet not completely full despite available food?

Penned pets automatically eat from trough when they need replenishment. They prioritize favorites, then foods with larger satiety gains; automatic feeding skips items exceeding missing satiety to avoid waste. A small deficit may remain. To fill it, open Cho ăn and click food. Manual feeding consumes final item even if some satiety exceeds maximum. Shift-click uses only one item, stopping if pet is already full.

## 5. Why did depositing one food take several stacks?

Clicking food in your inventory while Máng ăn is open deposits all matching stacks up to remaining capacity. It does not only deposit clicked stack. Food icons in trough section only display information. If item is rejected as food, use an exact food listed in trough. Capacity uses base satiety points; favorite bonus does not increase space an item occupies.

## 6. Why is Nạp từ ba lô missing?

Button appears only on your own ranch when server enables feature and TurtleBackpack is available. If clicking deposits nothing, backpack may lack valid food or trough may lack room for even one item. Visitors cannot fill someone else's trough.

## 7. Why does my pet not level up when I attack?

Pets earn combat EXP from damage and kills caused by their own projectiles. Your own kills do not directly grant pet EXP this way. Some foods also grant EXP; inspect EXP khi ăn line. Favorites increase satiety, not EXP multiplier. Pets at maximum level cannot gain further levels.

## 8. Why can my friend not see monsters, or why did they disappear?

Farm monsters belong to your personal encounter. They flee when lifetime expires; a fleeing boss grants no rewards. Changing worlds, teleporting far away, dying or leaving game can clear current encounter. Attack promptly and remain in farming area. Pets prioritize boss when present; by default Cáo Lửa attacks one target, Sói Lửa deals area damage and Phượng Hoàng Con attacks multiple targets (server may change these).

## 9. Can I visit offline ranches and help manage them?

You can visit offline owners if server can identify them and privacy permits it. Công khai allows everyone; Cùng đảo requires server-recognized shared island; Riêng tư is for owner. Visitors only inspect pets, trough and incubator; they cannot feed, rename, move or release pets. Opening your own hub does not itself end visit; use `/traithu thoat` to end it fully.

## 10. Which characters can pet names contain?

Use letters, digits, spaces, underscores or hyphens. Default limit is 16 characters (server may change it). Type name in anvil text field, then click result on right. Đặt lại tên gốc appears when pet has a custom name. Renaming costs no experience levels.

## 11. Does releasing a pet refund all food?

It does not refund all food consumed. Default refund is 5 Wheat per pet level into trough, limited by remaining capacity (server may change food and amount). Pet is permanently deleted after confirmation. If you only need pen space, use Rút về kho to keep pet.

## 12. Where do rewards go when inventory is full?

Plugin attempts delivery through reward system and mails items when needed. If notified of mailed rewards, check server mailbox. If that service is unavailable and inventory remains full, items may fail to arrive; leave room before farming. Currency rewards enter wallet when currency system supports that currency.
