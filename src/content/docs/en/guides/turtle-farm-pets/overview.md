## What does your pet ranch do?

TurtleFarmPets lets you raise pets that support your character. Penned pets grant buffs: effects that improve your stats or farming benefits. When you encounter this plugin's farm monsters, pets also fire support shots from ranch. You do not need to lead them beside you. Stored pets do not fight, grant buffs, or lose satiety over time.

Manage everything with `/traithu`: unlock pens, incubate eggs, feed pets, inspect stats and visit other ranches. Pen 1 starts unlocked by default; later pens unlock in order. Server may change prices and conditions. Read price shown on button before confirming purchase.

## Getting started step by step

1. Run `/traithu` to open **Trại pet của {player}**. Click **Tới trại** to reach ranch area and see pets directly. You can also click pens, trough or incubator there to open corresponding interface.
2. Once you have **Trứng Lửa**, keep it in your inventory. Open **Lò ấp**, then click egg icon to place one egg. Incubator holds one egg at a time. Trứng Lửa takes 30 minutes by default; server may change duration.
3. Wait for countdown to finish, then click ready egg in incubator. Incubation continues while you are offline. With ranch scene active and you online, a ready egg may also hatch automatically. New pet enters first empty pen; if none is available, look in **Kho pet**.
4. Open **Máng ăn** and click food in lower player inventory to deposit it. For example, clicking Wheat deposits matching wheat stacks up to capacity. Trough capacity uses satiety points, not inventory slots.
5. Click pet to inspect satiety, level, EXP and damage. EXP means experience that helps pet level up. Select **Cho ăn**, then click food to feed until full; hold Shift while clicking to use only one item.
6. For stored pets, click an empty pen to select one, or open pet details and choose **Đặt vào chuồng**. Then continue harvesting or cutting trees wherever server supports farm encounters.

## Your daily loop

Farm monsters appear by chance, and only you see your own monsters. You can attack them yourself while fed pets in pens automatically fire support shots. Defeating ordinary monsters advances boss summoning progress. When boss becomes weak, fed pets can perform **ĐÒN KẾT LIỄU!**. Defeat enemies before they flee to earn rewards; boss eggs are not guaranteed drops.

Pets earn EXP from damage and kills caused by their own projectiles, or from food that grants EXP. Damage changes with level according to species. After farming, check trough because pets automatically eat when satiety needs replenishing. Penned pets continue losing satiety while you are offline.

:::tip[Before a long break]
Withdraw pets to storage to stop satiety loss over time. Their buffs and combat support also stop until you place them back in pens.
:::
