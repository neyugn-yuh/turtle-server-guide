## What does TurtleLands do?

TurtleLands protects claimed territory, called a **land**. Claims use **chunks**, areas measuring 16 × 16 blocks horizontally. Menus manage membership, action permissions, travel destinations and environmental switches. Outsiders do not automatically gain rights to build, break blocks or open your chests.

A **role** is a member’s permission group. A **flag** is a switch for the whole land, such as PvP or fire spread. Claim fees come from the clicking player’s wallet; upkeep comes from the land’s separate bank. Check both before expanding.

## Getting started step by step

1. Find wilderness in a world where claiming is allowed. Use `/land map` and hover over cells for coordinates and blocking reasons. Occupied chunks and areas too close to protected zones cannot be claimed.
2. Stand where you want to create your land. If you neither own nor belong to any land, use `/land`, click **Create land**, check the price and type a name in chat, such as `Vuon Rua`. Names contain 3–24 characters: letters, numbers, spaces, `_` and `-`; first and last characters must be letters or numbers. Stay there until submitting the name.
3. Successful creation claims the first chunk and opens the hub. Hover over land information for chunk count and member limits. Defaults allow 1 owned land, 40 chunks per land and 10 counted members excluding the owner; server may change these limits.
4. Click **Claim map**, choose wilderness and read the confirmation before claiming. By default, new chunks must share an edge with existing claims in the same world; diagonals do not count. The first chunk in another world is exempt from adjacency.
5. Click **Members**, then **Invite player**; choose an online recipient and a role. Recipient must click **[Chấp nhận]** in chat and confirm. Invitations expire after 2 minutes by default; server may change this.
6. If server uses taxes, open **Bank and taxes**, deposit money and check the next collection. Money left in your wallet does not automatically pay land upkeep.

## Your regular routine

Check borders before building near claim edges. Expand when you can afford the claim and fund future upkeep. Grant container, breaking and management rights only to roles that need them. Use **Land flags** to check PvP, explosions, monsters and item pickup.

Stand inside your land and click **Set spawn**, if allowed. Later use `/land spawn`, select a land and stand still during warmup. Movement or damage cancels travel. Default warmup is 3 seconds; server may change it.

:::caution[Keep the bank funded]
Debt blocks expansion. Deposit enough to pay the current period before the displayed deadline. Overdue debt can disband your land and remove protection; default debt deadline is 7 days, which server may change.
:::
