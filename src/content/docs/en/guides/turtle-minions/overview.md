## What does a minion do?

A minion is a helper that produces resources automatically. Place a minion on your island and collect its products through its menu. Categories include mining, farming, foraging, fishing and combat. Products, speed and capacity depend on its type and tier. A tier is its upgrade level, usually shown with Roman numerals such as I and II.

Blocks and creatures around a minion are visual simulations. The minion does not mine real blocks. Production uses elapsed time, including time while you are offline, the area is unloaded or the server is shut down and later restarted. Full storage limits production.

## Getting started step by step

1. Obtain a minion item through your server's available methods. Read its description for its type, tier, products and storage capacity.
2. Go to your island and choose a spot where you can build and have a free minion placement slot. Hold the item and right-click the top face of a block to place it.
3. Right-click the minion to open its menu. For example, `Cobblestone Minion I` has one unlocked storage slot by default; the remaining white glass slots are unavailable. Your server may change these values.
4. Wait for products. Hover over the minion head to view the time between actions and its products. One production cycle consists of two actions, placing and breaking; the time between actions is not the duration of a whole cycle.
5. Click a product to collect that slot's entire stack, or click `Collect all` to collect everything, including attached storage. Make room in your inventory; anything that does not fit stays in storage.
6. When you have support items, open the menu and click an item in your inventory to install fuel, an upgrade item or a shipping hopper. You can also place it into the matching slot using your cursor.

:::caution[Not confirmed]
This guide does not know how your server hands out minions and support items (shop, crates, events...). Ask an administrator how to obtain them; ordinary player heads, barrels and hoppers do not substitute for the special items.
:::

## Maintaining and developing your minions

Fuel increases speed or multiplies products. For example, Coal normally provides 5% more speed for 30 minutes; your server may change this. Minions still produce without fuel. The two `Upgrade slot` spaces hold support items such as Auto Smelter or Compactor; these differ from the `Upgrade minion` button that increases the tier.

Place Minion Storage next to a minion to add storage. Each minion accepts only one attached storage. Automated Shipping sells products that no longer fit and holds the coins at the minion until you collect them.

Island members share the placement limit. By default, there are 5 base slots; recording a new type and tier combination for the first time can add one slot, up to 21 slots from this source. Placing the same type and tier again adds nothing. Your server may change limits or grant extra slots.

To move a minion, click `Pickup minion` or attack it. You receive the minion, products and support items back; temporary fuel is not refunded. Make room first, because returned items that do not fit will drop on the ground.
