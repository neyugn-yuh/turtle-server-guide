## What does TurtleHoes do?

TurtleHoes turns Harvester Hoe into a harvesting tool with lasting progression. Break farm crops with the hoe to gain produce, progress and rewards. Essence is the currency used to buy enchant levels: special effects attached to your hoe. Money, Shards and PlayerPoints are separate currencies; do not confuse them with Essence. Prices and available features depend on the server, so read each item description before buying.

## Getting started

1. Obtain Harvester Hoe through your server’s system. The plugin does not give you one on first join by default; ask an administrator how to obtain it if needed.
2. Put the hoe in your main hand and visit a field where harvesting is allowed. Play in survival mode; hoe harvesting does not apply in Creative.
3. Break mature crops. Immature crops cannot be harvested by default. Crops return through the server’s regrowth system; you do not need to plant replacement seeds.
4. Right-click to open Harvester Hoe, or use `/hoe`. Select an enchant to inspect its Essence price, maximum level and unlock requirements.
5. Buy a few levels first, keep harvesting and return for upgrades. Check Essence with `/essence` and your statistics with `/hoe stats`.

:::caution[Not confirmed]
How to obtain Harvester Hoe on your server is not confirmed. Ask an administrator; this guide does not confirm a hoe shop or crafting recipe.
:::

## Progression loop

Hoe level, attribute level and Farm Level are three different progressions. Hoe level does not increase automatically after enough crops: you must buy the upgrade. By default, clicking Upgrade Tool buys an attribute level with Money and Shards; Shift-click that button or use `/hoe levelup` to upgrade the hoe. This checks crops harvested by that specific hoe, Money and Essence.

Farm Level belongs to the player and increases through Farm XP earned while harvesting. Once eligible, use `/hoe prestige`, read the information and click twice to confirm. Prestige spends the required Farm XP, recalculates Farm Level from the remainder and increases bonuses. Hoe level stays unchanged. Enchants and Essence are also kept by default, but the server may change those two rules; check before confirming.

## Adding power

Buy and select active skills in Hoe Abilities. Shift-click an owned ability to select it, then hold the hoe and press Q by default, or use `/hoe ability fire`. Wait for its cooldown before using it again. The server may switch activation to F or commands only.

Crystals strengthen their matching enchants. Open Crystals to store, attempt applying or withdraw crystals. Apply chance does not guarantee success; failure still consumes the crystal. Mastery tracks successful triggers of eligible enchants: filling the progress bar earns Mastery Points, spent on bonuses for that specific hoe.

:::tip[Follow activities]
Use `/harvesterevent` to check the current contest and `/hoe boss` for boss progress or damage standings. Rewards and participation conditions depend on the server.
:::
