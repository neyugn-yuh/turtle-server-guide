## AutoSell

Use `/hoe autosell`. This menu appears only when the server uses PlayerPoints unlocking. By default, the command opens AutoSell Upgrade using the enchant upgrade layout.

![AutoSell](/gui/en/turtle-hoes-autosell.svg)

*Figure: Illustrative layout of AutoSell.*

| # | Slot | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 13 — Unlock AutoSell | Left-click to pay the displayed PlayerPoints price and enable; once unlocked, left-click to toggle. |
| 2 | 22 — Back | Click Back to return to Harvester Hoe. |

Slot 13 changes between Unlock AutoSell, AutoSell: Enabled and AutoSell: Disabled. One unlock covers all your hoes; enabling it again costs nothing.

## Tool Crystals

Click Crystals in Harvester Hoe.

![Tool Crystals](/gui/en/turtle-hoes-crystals.svg)

*Figure: Illustrative layout of Tool Crystals.*

| # | Slot | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 — Locked Crystal | Read hoe level and prestige requirements for more sockets. |
| 3 | 9 — Fortune Crystal | Normal mode: click to attempt applying; failure consumes the crystal. In Tinker/Withdraw: click to select or deselect. |
| 4 | 45 — Crystal Upgrades | Click to open Crystal Upgrades. |

| 6 | 47 — Back | Click Back to return to Harvester Hoe. |
| 7 | 48 — Previous Page | Click to change storage page. |
| 8 | 49 — Withdraw Crystals [Enter Mode] | Click to enter Withdraw; select crystals, then left-click this button to receive them as items. While active, right-click to cancel. |
| 9 | 50 — Next Page | Click to change storage page. |
| 10 | 52 — Crystal Filtering | Click to open Crystal Filtering. |
| 11 | 53 — Bulk Delete Crystals | Click to open Bulk Delete Crystals. |

Diagram shows a starting hoe with one stored crystal. Slots 1–8 change between equipped crystals, Inactive Crystal Slot and Locked Crystal. Storage uses slots 9–44 with pages; changing pages clears selection. By default, removing an equipped crystal requires Black Scroll. Click a crystal in your inventory below to store one at a time, only outside Tinker/Withdraw. The two modes cannot be combined.

## Prestige

Hold the hoe and use `/hoe prestige`.

![Prestige](/gui/en/turtle-hoes-prestige.svg)

*Figure: Illustrative layout of Prestige.*

| # | Slot | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 11 — Prestige {prestige}/{max} | View prestige, bonuses, Farm Level and Farm XP. |

Slot 15 shows Locked when Farm Level is insufficient or the limit is reached; otherwise it shows Prestige to {next}, then Click again to confirm. Review the effects before the second click.

## History - {player}

Use `/hoe history`; holding a hoe is unnecessary.

![History - {player}](/gui/en/turtle-hoes-history.svg)

*Figure: Illustrative layout of History - {player}.*

| # | Slot | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 — {text} | Example history entry; hover for time and type. Further entries occupy slots 1–44. |
| 2 | 49 — Showing: all | Click to cycle history types. |

Shows up to 45 latest matching entries without paging. Filter cycles through all, enchant, disenchant, essence, prestige, perk and ability. Icons vary by entry type; this command opens the menu without a Back button.
