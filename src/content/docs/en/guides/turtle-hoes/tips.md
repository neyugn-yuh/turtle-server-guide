## Why can’t I harvest a crop?

Hold the correct hoe in your main hand. Check that the crop is mature, has regrown and is in an allowed area. If crop locks are enabled, meet the hoe level, prestige and crop-total requirements. Owner restrictions can also prevent using someone else’s hoe.

## Why can friends still see crops I harvested?

By default, plugin farming zones track harvested crops separately for each player. You can share a field without taking another player’s crop turn. The server may use shared visibility instead.

## Why hasn’t my hoe leveled up after enough crops?

Hoe levels must be purchased. Use /hoe levelup or Shift-click Upgrade Tool when attributes are enabled. Crop progress belongs to that specific hoe; you still need Money and Essence. Farm Level is separate and rises automatically through Farm XP. Hoe level can also change crop tiers: defaults are level 5 for Enchanted Wheat, Enchanted Carrot and Enchanted Potato, and level 8 for Enchanted Sugar Cane, with higher sale values (the server may change these).

## Does prestige erase everything?

Prestige spends the required Farm XP, keeps the remainder and recalculates Farm Level. It does not change hoe level. Enchants and Essence are kept by default; the server may change those two rules. Ask an administrator before confirming if server rules are unpublished.

## How does AutoSell work?

By default, buy AutoSell with Essence on each hoe. The server can instead use an account unlock bought with PlayerPoints. AutoSell sells priced plugin crops from your inventory, not every kind of item. When enabled, periodic selling requires a Harvester Hoe in your inventory; the default interval is 60 seconds (the server may change it).

## Where do crops go when my inventory is full?

Overflow can be sold, dropped or discarded depending on the server. Selling is the default, but overflow sells only when AutoSell is enabled and the economy is available; otherwise it drops on the ground. Do not assume overflow always stays safely in your inventory.

## Where do I apply boosts, enhancements and crystals?

Close the plugin menu and open your personal inventory. Pick up the item on your cursor and click Harvester Hoe in your inventory. Valid items are consumed according to the application result. You can also store a crystal in Tool Crystals and click it there to try applying it.

## Why did a crystal disappear without a bonus?

Applying can fail and still consume the crystal. Each enchant accepts one crystal and requires an unlocked socket. By default, removal needs Black Scroll: apply it to the hoe to attempt removing a random crystal; read its success chance. Tinkering also destroys crystals and does not guarantee Dust.

## Why can’t I store or process crystals?

Filters reject crystals of filtered enchants, and full storage rejects deposits. Leave Tinker/Withdraw before clicking inventory crystals. Tinkering and crystal upgrades require the Dust/Shards currency system; ask an administrator if it is unavailable.

## Why won’t my ability fire, or why did a menu close?

Buy and select the ability, wait for its cooldown, then hold the hoe and use /hoe ability fire. Pending captcha verification also blocks automated harvesting. A hoe-bound menu closes on click if your main hand no longer holds the same hoe.

## What should I do when a boss appears?

Use /hoe boss to check progress or damage. When a boss appears, approach and hit it with the hoe in an eligible mode, outside Creative or Spectator. A private miniboss is visible and attackable only by its owner. Rewards follow the server’s boss rules.

## What if I withdraw Essence or crystals with a full inventory?

Items that do not fit drop at your location. Leave empty slots before withdrawing. Right-click an Essence Note to redeem it, one note per use.
